10 Key Comparison Structures
X is more/less ___ than Y
Spain's growth was more rapid than Italy's.
Both X and Y ___
Both countries showed growth, but Spain's was more pronounced.
X in contrast to Y ___
Spain's tourism, in contrast to Portugal's, increased sharply.
Compared to X, Y is ___
Compared to Portugal, Spain's figures were considerably higher.
One difference is that X ___ while Y ___
One difference is that Spain saw steady growth, while Portugal remained stable.
X overtook Y in ___
Spain's visitor numbers overtook France's in 2015.
The most important difference is ___
The most important difference is that Spain grew while others declined.
X is not as ___ as Y
Germany's growth was not as rapid as Spain's during this period.
While X ___, Y ___
While Spain increased by 50%, Italy remained relatively flat.
The ___ is X; the ___ is Y
The highest point was 8.5M; the lowest was 2.1M.
⚠️ Critical rule: Superlatives always need THE — "the highest," "the most rapid," "the most volatile," "the sharpest"

✓ Noun phrase rule: Use "Spain's tourism" or "the tourism of Spain" — NOT just "Spain." Use "the GDP of India" or "India's GDP" — NOT "India's GDP was higher than China" because you're comparing countries, not the objects themselves.
