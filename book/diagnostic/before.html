Mastering Line Graphs · Before you start

Which Task 1 skills should you practise first?

An 8-minute diagnostic designed by a former IELTS examiner. Do 12 quick tasks on one line graph and get a suggested starting point. It checks what you know about Task 1; it is not a band estimate. At the end of the course, a second version on a new graph shows how far you’ve come.
