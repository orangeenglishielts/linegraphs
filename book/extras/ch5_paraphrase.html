Paraphrase without changing the meaning

Choose a word or phrase for each gap that fits both the meaning and the grammar of the sentence.

How to use this. Some original words are best kept: you do not need to replace every word. Museum visitors are not necessarily tourists, and a museum is not always a gallery. Accuracy matters more than avoiding repetition.

Choose an option in each gap. You'll see straight away whether it is accurate, possible but less precise, or whether it changes the meaning or doesn't fit, and why. You can change your choice at any time.
