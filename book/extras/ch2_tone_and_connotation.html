Band 7+

Choosing verbs with the right tone

Why “sales boomed” works and “mortality boomed” doesn’t, and how to use strong verbs without losing marks.

Why tone matters at Band 7

At Band 7, the examiner looks for less common vocabulary used with awareness of style and collocation. Strong verbs like boomed, slumped or spiralled can show that range, but each one carries a feeling. If the feeling doesn’t match the topic, the word becomes an error, and a less common word used wrongly costs more than a simple one used well.

The test: before you use a strong verb, ask whether more of this thing is good news or bad news. Then check that your verb says the same.

Three kinds of trend verb

Neutral · always saferose, increased, grew, fell, declined, dropped, remained stable, fluctuatedAdd an adverb for size and speed: rose slightly, fell sharply, increased dramatically.
Size and speed · no judgementsoared, surged, rocketed, plummeted, plunged, doubled, halvedThey say how big and how fast, not whether it was good. “Sales soared” and “unemployment soared” are both fine.
Evaluative · carries a feelingPositive: boomed, flourished, thrived, recovered, rallied, picked up
Negative: slumped, crashed, collapsed, spiralled, deteriorated, stagnatedUse these only when the feeling matches the topic, and only for changes big enough to deserve them.

Improved and worsened work best for a situation: “literacy improved”, “air quality worsened”. With a figure, they can hide which way the number moved. “The illiteracy rate improved” makes the reader work it out, so write that it fell.
No opinions. Words like fortunately, worryingly or sadly give your view of the data. Task 1 asks you to report it, so leave them out even when the topic is serious.

Rewrite this paragraph so every verb matches its topic and the size of the change.

Between 2000 and 2020, deaths from road accidents in the country flourished from 4,000 to 6,500 a year, while the number of cars on the roads boomed from 10 million to 18 million. Fortunately, the death rate then recovered to 5,000 by 2022.
Between 2000 and 2020, deaths from road accidents in the country rose significantly, from 4,000 to 6,500 a year, while the number of cars on the roads increased by 80%, from 10 million to 18 million. By 2022, the number of deaths had fallen to 5,000.

Before you use a strong verb

  1. Which way did the line move, and how far? Calculate it.
  2. Is more of this thing good news or bad news?
  3. Does my verb carry a feeling? Does it match?
  4. Not sure? Use a neutral verb with an adverb. It is always safe.
