    Describe your day

    Write a short paragraph about your line for someone who can’t see it. Select the main features, describe the changes with the words from this page, and compare different parts of the day.

    The graph shows how happy one person felt at different times on a normal day. My happiness was at its lowest point at 6 am, when I woke up, but it rose sharply after breakfast, reaching 7 by 9 am. It then remained stable until lunchtime before dipping slightly in the afternoon. In the evening, it climbed steadily and peaked at 9 at 9 pm, more than three times as high as at the start of the day, before falling back to 6 at midnight.

    Remember

    1. Picture the line before you choose the word.
    2. Check the size and speed: marginal or dramatic, steadily or sharply?
    3. After a rise or fall, say what happened next: did it level off, peak, bottom out or recover?
