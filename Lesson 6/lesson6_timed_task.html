Section 4: Peer Feedback
Get a second pair of eyes on your response — and practise the checklist on someone else's
  1. Post your finished response in this lesson's Discussion below.
  2. Read two classmates' responses.
  3. For each one, check the four things below and leave one specific comment.
When reviewing a classmate's response, check:
  • Does the introduction paraphrase the question (not copy it)?
  • Is the overview free of numbers?
  • Are the body paragraphs grouped logically?
  • Is the tense appropriate for the time frame shown?
Useful starters for your comment:
I like how you...One thing I'd check is...Have you considered...Your strongest paragraph was... because...
Spotting someone else's mistakes is one of the fastest ways to stop making your own.
