The Four-Part Shape
1. Introduction — one sentence
Paraphrase the question. Change the structure and some vocabulary, but keep every category from the original.
2. Overview — one to two sentences
Summarise the two or three biggest patterns, in general terms only. No numbers.
3. Body Paragraph 1 — with figures
First logical group of data, grouped by category or by time period.
4. Body Paragraph 2 — with figures
Remaining data, continuing the same grouping logic as Body Paragraph 1.
⚠️ Critical rule: the overview comes straight after the introduction and contains no numbers — every figure is saved for the body paragraphs.
Useful Phrases by Part
Phrase Example in context
Introduction
The line graph illustrates……how many people visited three London museums.
The chart shows……the revenue of three coffee shop chains.
…over the period from X to Y…over the 30-year period from 1990 to 2020.
Overview
Overall, …Overall, ownership increased across all age groups.
In general, …In general, both cities saw passenger numbers rise.
It is clear that…It is clear that one chain grew far faster than the rest.
Body paragraphs
Specifically, …Specifically, the 18–30 age group reached 95% by 2020.
In contrast, …In contrast, the 65+ age group grew far more slowly.
Meanwhile, …Meanwhile, Chain C grew only modestly.
Linking the parts
While X…, Y…While City A climbed steadily, City B fluctuated.
By the end of the period, …By the end of the period, both cities had overtaken their 2000 figures.
✓ Grouping rule: split body paragraphs by category (e.g. similar items together) or by time period (e.g. first half vs second half) — pick whichever makes the comparison clearest for your specific graph.
