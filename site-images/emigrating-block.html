Moving abroad from the Netherlands or Belgium? Vertrekklaar.nl

Passing IELTS is one step of an emigration. For Australia, Canada or New Zealand there is a lot more to arrange, on both sides of the move.

Vertrekklaar.nl, built by Auke Puyt, is a personal step-by-step emigration plan for about 70 destinations, for people leaving the Netherlands or Belgium. For Australia, Canada and New Zealand it takes you through the points system: the skills assessment, the documents, the police certificates and the language test, including what your score earns you in points and when to book it. It also covers everything at home: deregistering, health insurance, pension, tax and the move, with ready-made letters and a calendar for your first year abroad.

Every step links to the official source, the plans are checked every two weeks, and you get a message when a rule changes. The first phase and a free checklist are open to everyone at Vertrekklaar.nl.
