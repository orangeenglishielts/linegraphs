Practise Your IELTS Line Graph Overview: 3 Interactive Activities

By Lindsey, former IELTS examiner. 20 November 2026. Read this post with its interactive exercises.

In IELTS Writing Task 1, a clear overview of the main trends is part of what the Task Achievement criterion looks for at Band 7 and above. It is also the paragraph many candidates find hardest to write. As a former IELTS examiner, I'd suggest practising it in three small steps: read the graph, name the trends, then build the overview.

Below are three short interactive activities on one line graph. They take about five minutes, you get feedback at every step, and you can retry as often as you like.

The graph

The line graph shows the number of visitors to three museums in Riverton between 2000 and 2020. Riverton is an invented town, so you can focus on the trends.

Try the three activities

  1. Read the graph. Four quick questions that make you look closely before you write.
  2. Match the trends. Drag phrases such as "rose sharply" and "levelled off" onto the right part of each line.
  3. Build the overview. Complete a model overview, then listen to me explain why it works.

Open the three activities

On a phone, turn it sideways or tap the full-screen arrows in the corner of the activity.

Prefer pen and paper?

Here are the same tasks to do in a notebook.

Activity 1: Read the graph

  1. Which museum had the most visitors in 2000?
  2. Which line changes direction most often?
  3. When did the History Museum reach its highest point?
  4. Which museum had the most visitors at the end of the period?

Answers: 1 the History Museum. 2 the Art Gallery. 3 2010. 4 the Science Museum.

Activity 2: Match the trends

Match each phrase to one part of the graph. One phrase is not needed.

rose sharply · levelled off · peaked · fell steadily · fluctuated · plummeted

  • A: the Science Museum from 2006 to 2012
  • B: the Science Museum after 2014
  • C: the History Museum in 2010
  • D: the History Museum from 2010 to 2020
  • E: the Art Gallery across the whole period

Answers: A rose sharply. B levelled off. C peaked. D fell steadily. E fluctuated. "Plummeted" is not needed, because the History Museum's fall is gradual, not sudden.

Activity 3: Build the overview

Complete the overview with these words. Two are not needed.

Overall · rose · most popular · In contrast · declined · fluctuated · stable · Firstly · plummeted

"_____, the number of visitors to the Science Museum _____ significantly, and by the end of the period it had become the _____ of the three museums. _____, the History Museum, which was initially the most visited, _____ in the second half of the period, while visitor numbers at the Art Gallery _____ but remained relatively _____."

Answers: Overall, rose, most popular, In contrast, declined, fluctuated, stable.

Why this overview works

This overview works because it gives the big picture without any numbers. It starts with "Overall", so the reader knows straight away what this paragraph is doing. It picks out the most important change, the Science Museum's sharp rise, and then compares the other two museums using "In contrast" and "while". Notice there are no figures here. Save those for your body paragraphs.

Now write your own

Cover the model and write your own two-sentence overview of the same graph. Then compare the two: did you include the biggest change, a comparison, and no figures?
