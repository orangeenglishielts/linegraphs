Prepositions for Describing Data in IELTS Writing Task 1

By Lindsey, former IELTS examiner. 17 November 2026. Read this post with its interactive exercises.

Getting prepositions right in IELTS Writing Task 1 makes your descriptions clearer and more accurate. These small but mighty words: at, by, to, from, in: are the building blocks that connect your data descriptions smoothly and accurately. Master them, and your graph descriptions will flow naturally while demonstrating the grammatical range examiners expect at higher bands.

Let's dive into the essential preposition patterns that will transform your Task 1 writing from good to exceptional.

Understanding the Big Four: From, To, By, and At

FROM and TO: Your Navigation Tools

Think of FROM and TO as your GPS coordinates for data movement. They show exactly where numbers start and where they end up.

Perfect for showing complete journeys:

  • Sales increased from 50 units to 200 units between January and March
  • The unemployment rate dropped from 8.5% to 6.2% over the five-year period
  • Internet usage rose from 45% to 78% of the population

Quick Quiz 1: Complete these sentences with FROM or TO:

  1. The population grew _____ 2 million _____ 3.5 million
  2. House prices fell _____ $400,000 in 2020
  3. Student numbers increased _____ 1,500 _____ 2,200 during the decade

Answers: 1. from/to, 2. to, 3. from/to

BY: The Change Measurer

BY tells us exactly how much something changed. It's your precision tool for showing the magnitude of movement.

Use BY when specifying the amount of change:

  • Profits increased by $2 million
  • The temperature rose by 3 degrees
  • Exports fell by 15% last quarter

Note: With percentages, BY is optional: "Sales increased by 30%" or "Sales increased 30%" both work perfectly.

AT: The Position Marker

AT pinpoints exact locations on your data map: starting points, ending points, or steady positions.

Starting positions:

  • The graph began at 100 units
  • Sales started at their lowest point

Ending positions:

  • The line finished at 250 units
  • Unemployment ended at 4.2%

Steady positions:

  • The rate remained constant at 25%
  • Population stayed stable at around 50,000

Time Prepositions That Actually Make Sense

IN: Your Time Container

Use IN for specific periods that contain your data points:

  • Months: in January, in December
  • Years: in 2019, in 2023
  • Quarters: in the first quarter, in Q3

Examples in action:

  • Smartphone sales peaked in November
  • The highest unemployment rate occurred in 2008
  • Consumer confidence was strongest in the fourth quarter

BETWEEN...AND vs FROM...TO

Both show time ranges, but they're not interchangeable:

  • BETWEEN 2010 AND 2015 ✓
  • FROM 2010 TO 2015 ✓
  • BETWEEN 2010 TO 2015 ✗ (Never mix them!)

DURING and OVER: The Period Describers

These show things happening within a timeframe without pinpointing exact start/end dates:

  • DURING the recession, sales remained low
  • OVER the past decade, technology adoption accelerated

Practice Exercise 1: Choose the correct preposition:

  1. Sales fluctuated wildly (during/at) the summer months
  2. The company expanded rapidly (between/from) 2018 (and/to) 2020
  3. Profits reached their peak (in/at) March
  4. The trend continued (over/by) several years

Answers: 1. during, 2. between/and, 3. in, 4. over

The OF vs IN Decision Tree

This distinction trips up many test-takers, but there's a simple rule:

Use OF to give the size of the change:

  • An increase of 500 students
  • A drop of 12 percentage points
  • A rise of $50 per barrel

Use IN to say what changed:

  • An increase in student enrolment
  • A drop in oil prices
  • A rise in consumer confidence

Memory Trick: OF gives the amount, IN names what changed. You can use both: an increase of 500 in student enrolment.

Advanced Preposition Patterns

Combining Prepositions for Sophistication

Starting AT and ending AT: "Online shopping began at 15% of total retail sales and climbed steadily, finishing at 35% by 2023."

FROM...TO with BY amounts: "Manufacturing output increased from 2,000 units to 3,500 units, representing a growth of 1,500 units."

Complex time and data combinations: "Between 2015 and 2020, renewable energy consumption rose from 12% to 28% of total energy use, an increase of 16 percentage points over the five-year period."

Common Preposition Pitfalls (And How to Avoid Them)

Mistake 1: Wrong preposition for amounts

  • ❌ "Increased at 30%"
  • ✅ "Increased by 30%" or "Increased to 30%"

Mistake 2: Mixing time prepositions

  • ❌ "Between 2010 to 2015"
  • ✅ "Between 2010 and 2015" or "From 2010 to 2015"

Mistake 3: Unclear starting vs ending points

  • ❌ "Sales increased to $100 by starting at $50"
  • ✅ "Sales increased from $50 to $100"

Comprehensive Quiz 2: Fix the preposition errors:

  1. Profits rose at 25% between January to March
  2. The population increased of 200,000 people
  3. Sales started from $1 million and ended to $2 million
  4. There was a drop of unemployment during 2019

Corrected answers: 1. Profits rose by 25% between January and March 2. The population increased by 200,000 people 3. Sales started at $1 million and ended at $2 million 4. There was a drop in unemployment during 2019

Practice Makes Perfect: Real Task 1 Applications

Let's put these prepositions to work with authentic Task 1 language:

Chart showing smartphone sales 2018-2022: "Smartphone sales began at 2.5 million units in 2018 and grew steadily over the four-year period, reaching 4.2 million by 2022. The most significant growth occurred between 2020 and 2021, when sales increased from 3.1 million to 3.8 million units, representing an increase of 700,000 units in just one year."

Graph displaying temperature changes: "Temperatures fluctuated considerably during the summer months, starting at 22°C in June and peaking at 35°C in August. The most dramatic change occurred from July to August, when the average temperature rose by 8 degrees."

Final Challenge Exercise

Complete this Task 1 description using appropriate prepositions:

"The bar chart shows coffee consumption ___ two countries ___ 2020 ___ 2023. Brazil started ___ the highest consumption rate ___ 45 cups per person per month ___ 2020. ___ the three-year period, consumption increased ___ both countries, with Brazil rising ___ 45 to 52 cups, an increase ___ 7 cups. Norway showed the most dramatic growth, jumping ___ 12 cups ___ 28 cups, representing an increase ___ 16 cups ___ just three years."

Answer Key: in, from, to, with, at, in, Over, in, from, of, from, to, of, in

Your Next Steps to Preposition Mastery

Prepositions might seem small, but they carry big weight in IELTS Task 1. Focus on these key strategies:

  1. Practise the FROM/TO pattern daily - it appears in almost every Task 1 response
  2. Master the OF vs IN distinction for describing increases and decreases
  3. Use AT confidently for starting points, ending points, and steady states
  4. Perfect your time prepositions - they show your command of English structure

Remember, consistent preposition accuracy demonstrates the grammatical range and precision that IELTS examiners look for. Start incorporating these patterns into your practice responses, and watch your Task 1 descriptions become more natural, precise, and impressive.
